## The seven income streams, in priority order

### 1. Paid sponsored content and press releases (fastest cash, start this week)
African fintechs, exchanges, remittance apps and PR agencies routinely pay publications to carry press releases and sponsored articles. Benchmarks from comparable outlets:

| Outlet | Sponsored post | Press release |
|---|---|---|
| [Techeconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Techeconomy-2026-Media-Kit.pdf) (up to 90k monthly users) | ₦165,000 / $110 | ₦132,000 / $88 |
| [Techtrends Africa](https://techtrends.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Techrends-Africa-Media-Kit-Updated-June-2026-.pdf) | ₦125,000 / $95 | ₦125,000 / $95 |

| [TechCabal (Big Cabal Media)](https://www.scribd.com/document/1009456920/Big-Cabal-Media-Kit-January-2026) | ₦225,750 post, ₦486,975 custom article | |

Rules to protect credibility (and Google rankings):
- Every paid post is labelled "Sponsored" or "Partner Content", carries `rel="sponsored"` on outbound links, and goes in a separate Sponsored category, never in news categories.
- Paid content must meet the same accuracy standard. No paid "news" that misleads readers, no guaranteed-return crypto schemes, no unlicensed investment offers.
- Editorial news coverage is never for sale.

### 2. Direct banner sponsorships
Sell fixed weekly placements (homepage leaderboard, sidebar, in-article) to fintechs, exchanges and remittance companies targeting Africa and the diaspora. These pay far more per impression than AdSense and are booked directly.

### 3. Google AdSense (passive baseline)
The code is already on the site. Steps: confirm the account is approved and serving, make sure `ads.txt` is live with the AdSense line, enable Auto ads with moderate density (to protect PageSpeed), and keep ads off Sponsored posts. Once traffic passes roughly 10,000 sessions a month, apply to Ezoic or Journey by Mediavine, which typically pay more than AdSense.

### 4. Daily email newsletter ("Fintech Telex Daily Brief")
A newsletter turns one-time visitors into a daily audience you own. Recommended platform: beehiiv (free to start), whose Ad Network and Boosts programmes pay publishers for sponsored placements and for recommending other newsletters. The daily automation can generate the brief from the day's 11 posts. Newsletter sponsorships then become a sellable product.

### 5. Affiliate partnerships (disclosed)
Join affiliate or referral programmes from remittance apps and regulated exchanges popular with African and diaspora readers, then add disclosed links in relevant guides and a "Best ways to send money to Nigeria/Ghana/Kenya" evergreen page. Only promote licensed providers, label every affiliate link, and never place affiliate links inside news stories.

### 6. PR agency distribution listings
Nigerian PR firms resell placements on publication rate cards (for example [Evaluate PR's official rate card](https://evaluate.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Official-Rate-Card.pdf) lists 30+ outlets). Getting Fintech Telex on these lists brings steady paid press releases without cold outreach.

### 7. Later: reports, events and jobs
Once the audience grows: sponsored quarterly reports (for example "State of African Crypto Regulation"), webinars and a fintech jobs board.
