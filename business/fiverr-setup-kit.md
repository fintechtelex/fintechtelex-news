# Fintech Telex on Fiverr: Setup Kit

Ready-to-paste copy for the Fiverr freelancer profile and the first four gigs. A new seller can list up to 4 gigs.

## Rules that shape this kit

- **Fiverr payments only.** Every order must be paid through Fiverr. Gigs, messages and deliveries must never mention PayPal, OduwaCoin (OWC) wallet addresses, editor@fintechtelex.com, phone numbers or the Advertise page as a way to pay. Fiverr can suspend accounts that move business off the platform.
- **Contact after an order only.** Talk to buyers in Fiverr messages. Third-party tools are allowed only after an order is placed.
- **No unsolicited messages.** Do not cold-message Fiverr users to promote gigs.
- **One account, real identity.** Use one account in your real name or your business name. Verify it with your own ID.
- **No guaranteed results.** Do not promise rankings, traffic, backlinks that pass SEO value, or price moves.
- **No crypto trading services.** Do not offer buying, selling or trading crypto, trading signals, or token promotion that could cause financial harm.
- **Writing and publishing are allowed.** News, press releases and sponsored articles about crypto and fintech are fine. Every crypto client post must pass our editorial check.
- **Fiverr's fee.** Fiverr keeps 20% of each order. The prices below are about 25% above the Advertise page rates so your net stays close to those rates.

- **Display name:** your real first name plus last initial, or your registered business name. Do not include words like "expert" or "best".

- **Skills:** Press Release Writing, Article Writing, News Writing, Fintech, Cryptocurrency, Blockchain, Content Editing, Public Relations
