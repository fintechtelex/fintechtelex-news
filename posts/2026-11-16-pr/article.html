November 16, 2026 — Fintech Telex, the Africa- and diaspora-focused news and media platform, today announced that it now accepts OduwaCoin (OWC), the native cryptocurrency of the Pan-African OduwaCoin Layer 1 blockchain, as payment for paid publication and advertising. Advertisers, PR agencies and brands can now book sponsored articles, press releases, banner placements and partner packages and pay in OWC or in US dollars via PayPal.

Disclosure: Fintech Telex and OduwaCoin share common ownership.

What advertisers can pay for in OWC

All Fintech Telex commercial products are priced in US dollars and can be settled in OWC. The current rate card on the Advertise With Us page includes:

  • Press release: $60
  • Sponsored article: $120
  • Spotlight feature or founder interview: $200
  • Sidebar banner (300x250): $50 per week
  • Homepage leaderboard (728x90): $80 per week
  • Launch bundle: $250
  • Monthly partner package: $600 per month

How OWC payment works

  1. The advertiser emails editor@fintechtelex.com with the product and preferred dates.
  2. Fintech Telex issues an invoice in US dollars, together with the OWC equivalent calculated from the live OWC/USD price on CoinGecko on the invoice date. Each OWC quote is valid for 24 hours.
  3. The advertiser sends OWC to the Fintech Telex payment address shown on the invoice and on the Advertise page, then emails the transaction ID.
  4. Once the payment is confirmed on the OduwaCoin blockchain, the content or campaign goes live on the agreed date.

For example, at the CoinGecko price of about $0.24 per OWC on November 16, 2026, a $120 sponsored article would cost roughly 500 OWC. The exact amount is set on the invoice date.

Why OduwaCoin

Many of Fintech Telex's readers and advertisers are African businesses and diaspora professionals who face high card fees, currency controls and slow cross-border transfers. Accepting OWC gives them a direct, borderless way to pay for media services from anywhere, alongside the existing PayPal option for US dollar payments.

Editorial standards stay the same

Paying in OWC does not change Fintech Telex's editorial rules. All paid content is clearly labelled as "Sponsored" or "Partner Content", and news coverage is never for sale. Fintech Telex does not accept promotions for unlicensed investment schemes, guaranteed-return offers or misleading claims, whatever payment method is used. Readers can find the full policy on the Advertise With Us page.

About Fintech Telex

Fintech Telex (fintechtelex.com) publishes daily news and analysis on finance, crypto, business, security, artificial intelligence, technology, Nigeria news and politics, sports, entertainment and the African diaspora, as well as the Fintech Spotlight how-to series.

About OduwaCoin

OduwaCoin (OWC) is a Pan-African Layer 1 blockchain with its own native coin, open-source wallet and node software, and proof-of-stake staking. OWC market data is listed on CoinGecko.
