BENIN CITY, Edo State, November 19, 2026 — Dr. Bright Enabulele, the technology entrepreneur who stood as the Accord Party's candidate in Edo State's 2024 governorship election, has resigned his membership of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) ahead of Nigeria's 2027 general election.

Disclosure: Fintech Telex and OduwaCoin share common ownership. Dr. Bright Enabulele is a co-founder and CEO of OduwaCoin. This is a press release.

Dr. Enabulele said he left because of differences in ideology with the party and over how it handles merit in choosing who should lead Edo State. He said the party had failed many potential aspirants who have the goodwill of the people.

"I have taken this decision because my ideology and the direction of the party no longer agree. I believe the right to lead Edo State should be earned on merit and on the goodwill of the people. Sadly, the party has failed many aspirants who have that goodwill," Dr. Enabulele said.

He left on good terms with former colleagues:

"I wish every candidate and every friend contesting on the NDC platform good luck."

Context in Edo

Dr. Enabulele's exit comes weeks after members of the NDC's Edo State chapter protested alleged irregularities in the party's primaries, and the party appealed for calm (BusinessDay). The NDC was founded in 2026 (Wikipedia) and has since unveiled its Edo Senate and House of Representatives candidates for 2027 (Daily Trust).

From blockchain entrepreneur to Edo candidate

Dr. Enabulele built his public profile in technology before entering politics. He is the founder of OduwaCoin, a Pan-African Layer 1 blockchain project, and he also leads the WakaMap navigation platform. In an NTA profile, he explained his move into politics this way: "No matter how powerful your technology is, without leadership alignment, impact is limited."

Key milestones in his political career:

  • February 17, 2024: Kennedy Iyere won the Accord Party's Edo governorship primary and named Dr. Enabulele, a US-based entrepreneur, software engineer and blockchain specialist, as his running mate (The Guardian Nigeria).
  • September 2024: The Social Democratic Party (SDP) adopted Dr. Enabulele as its candidate in a joint arrangement with the Accord Party (NigeriaWorld).
  • September 20, 2024: Iyere withdrew less than 24 hours before the poll, and the Accord Party named Dr. Enabulele its governorship candidate (Vanguard).
  • October 2024: He took the result of the September 21 election to the election petition tribunal, alleging irregularities (Vanguard).
  • April 2025: The tribunal sitting in Abuja dismissed the petition and upheld Governor Monday Okpebholo's victory (Independent Nigeria).
  • May 2025: He published "The Man—Dr Bright Enabulele," a book about his move from blockchain entrepreneur to political leader (PRLog).
  • August 2025: He resigned from the Accord Party and said he would consult his stakeholders on his next move (Vanguard).
  • November 2026: He resigned from the NDC ahead of the 2027 general election.

Dr. Enabulele did not name a new political platform. He said he would announce his next steps later.
